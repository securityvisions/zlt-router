# FTTH Deployment Map: Ponak Faraz Park 12-Unit Complex

## Destination
A validated, field-tested operational playbook and tactical roadmap for the building manager of the 12-unit complex at Faraz Park (Ponak) to secure TCI Tanoma FTTH installation, based on verified community reviews (Google Maps, Twitter/X, forums) and known contractor friction points.

## Notes
- Location: Faraz Park, Farvardin St corner, Ponak (District 5).
- Infrastructure: Internal riser cabling already finished; needs drop cable + FAT box.
- Active skills: `wayfinder`, `camofox`.
- Preference: TCI Tanoma (Dynamic Public IPv4, low GB pricing, bridge ONT to Xiaomi AX3000T).

## Decisions so far
- [Telecom Center Jurisdiction](issues/01-telecom-center-jurisdiction.md): "ایران‌زمین" refers to مرکز مخابرات پیام نور (ایران زمین جنوبی/مخبری). Physical line termination for Faraz Park is under مرکز مخابرات شهید نظری (سردار جنگل).
- [Tanoma Application Roadmap Baseline](issues/02-tanoma-application-roadmap.md): Collective petition from residents is required to hit the contractor's feasibility threshold.
- [Google Maps Reviews Deep Intel](issues/03-google-maps-reviews-intel.md): Verified that single applicants wait months because contractors demand multi-unit economies of scale; 12 units is the exact antidote.
- [Public Social & Forum Intelligence](issues/04-twitter-community-sentiment.md): Verified online coverage maps are out-of-sync; field contractor dispatch and multi-unit petition are the only reliable route; opting out of operator modems speeds up delivery.
- [Contractor Friction & Bureaucracy Bypass Tactics](issues/05-bureaucracy-bypass-tactics.md): 5 actionable tactics to bypass contractor excuses and fast-track FAT deployment.
- [Video Transcript & Deep Community Extraction](issues/06-video-transcript-analysis.md): Verbatim transcript analysis from 1-year field video review, confirming 360-400M real speed, Hysteria2/Reality stability, and Commercial Tanoma tariff options.
