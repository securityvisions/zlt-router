# 01 - Tariffs & Pricing Deep-Dive: Shatel vs TCI Tanoma

**Type:** `research`  
**Status:** resolved

## Resolution

1. **Initial Entry Bundles & Setup:**
   - **Shatel Fiber:** Promotional entry bundles (e.g. 6-month Extreme with 2,400 GB international traffic + ONT modem included + free setup) cost **~20,015,000 Toman** (or 3-month Premium with 300 GB for **~15,363,000 Toman**). Shatel offers installment payment on bundles.
   - **TCI Tanoma:** Charges setup fees via telephone bill (~50,000 Toman/month). Customers can buy their own ONT modem from the open market (e.g. Huawei HG8010H bridge ONT for ~1,800,000 to 2,500,000 Toman).
2. **Per-Gigabyte & Renewal Economics:**
   - **TCI Tanoma:** Base plans start at **190,000 Toman/month** for 45 GB international (121.5 GB domestic) on 100M–1000M profile. Extra international traffic averages **~1,500 to 2,000 Toman / GB**.
   - **Shatel Fiber:** Extra international traffic averages **~1,800 to 2,400 Toman / GB**.
   - **Postpaid Advantage:** TCI allows postpaid billing directly on the fixed landline phone bill (پس‌پرداخت روی قبض تلفن). Shatel requires upfront wallet/portal renewals.
3. **Winner on Tariffs & Upfront Cost:**
   - **TCI Tanoma wins on base pricing & flexibility:** Much cheaper monthly baseline and allows bringing your own cheap bridge ONT.
   - **Shatel wins on high-volume promotional bundles:** 2400 GB for 6 months at 20M Toman (including modem) is very cost-effective for heavy users.  
**Blocked by:** none
